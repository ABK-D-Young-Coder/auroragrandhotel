The Aurora story

International luxury, grounded in local character.

Aurora Grand Hotel was imagined as a modern landmark for Jos: a place where guests can arrive from anywhere in the world and feel immediately at ease.

Our philosophy is simple: excellent design matters, but the way a guest is welcomed matters more.

What guides us

Mission. Values. Hospitality.

Mission

To create exceptional stays through thoughtful design, genuine care and consistently polished service.

Values

Warmth, integrity, craft, respect for place and attention to details that make people feel considered.

Philosophy

Luxury should feel effortless. Our role is to make the experience memorable without making it complicated.
